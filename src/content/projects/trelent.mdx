My day job. I'm an AI Forward Deployed Developer at Trelent. We build private AI
platforms, the kind that run in the client's own environment, not someone
else's cloud.

Picture a financial analyst copying data between three systems, every day, for
years. That's the kind of workflow I get sent in to blow up. Different clients,
same problem: valuable people doing repetitive things.

I sit between the AI layer and the interface people actually use. I design the
integrations, build the surfaces, and make sure the automation feels like it
belongs rather than bolted on.
