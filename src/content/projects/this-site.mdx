The globe up top is just a 2D canvas. No WebGL, no 3D library, only a bit of math
that wraps a list of words around a sphere and spins it every frame. *Go ahead,
grab it.* Give it a good fling and it keeps spinning, then slowly settles back
to its own pace.

The colored words are the fun part. Each one is a note on the wall below. As you
scroll and a note's spot comes into view, its word lets go of the globe and drops
into place, with a little bounce when it lands. Scroll back up and they fly home.
Not in the mood to scroll? Click a colored word and it takes you straight to its
note.

I know a messy wall isn't for everyone, so there's a **tidy up** button that lines
everything up. **mess it up** puts it all back. :)

If your device asks for reduced motion, nothing flies around and everything is
already in place. And with JavaScript off, you still get a plain grid you can
read.
