The globe is a plain 2D canvas. No WebGL and no 3D library. The words sit on a
fibonacci sphere and get projected by hand every frame.

- Drag it and it keeps the spin you gave it, then eases back to its own drift.
- Scroll and it shrinks into the corner. Once its words get too small to read,
  they turn into dots.
- Each colored word is a note. When the note's spot on the wall scrolls into view,
  it lets go of the globe and falls there on a spring, so it bounces a little
  when it lands. Scroll back up and it flies home.
- Click a colored word and the page takes you to its note.
- **tidy up** moves every note into a neat grid, one after another. **mess it up**
  puts them back.

With reduced motion on, the globe holds still and the notes are already in
place. With JavaScript off, the wall is a plain grid you can still read.
